World Cup 2026: Brazil Draw with Morocco, Australia Stun Turkey as Scotland Claim Historic Win

Five-time champions Brazil were held to a 1-1 stalemate, the USA dominated Paraguay, and Scotland ended a 36-year World Cup win drought in an action-packed Saturday of Group Stage football.

WorldCupDaily Staff · Saturday, June 13, 2026 · 5 min read

Packed stadiums across the United States delivered an electric atmosphere on a memorable Saturday of World Cup football. (Getty Images)

Saturday, June 13, delivered everything a World Cup fan could ask for: a blockbuster draw between heavyweights Brazil and Morocco, a major upset as Australia dismantled Turkey, Scotland's first World Cup win in 36 years, and a last-gasp equalizer that denied Switzerland in the opening game of the day. Here is everything that happened.

Qatar
1 – 1
Switzerland
Group B · Lumen Field, Seattle
Qatar Rescue Dramatic Draw Against Switzerland

Switzerland appeared to be heading for a comfortable opening win when they went ahead in the first half, controlling possession and limiting Qatar's attacking threat. But the host nation of the 2022 World Cup refused to be put to the sword.

Deep into stoppage time, with Switzerland's players already celebrating a victory, Qatar captain Boualem Khoukhi rose highest from a corner to head home a stunning 90+5 equaliser — silencing the Swiss and sending the Qatari supporters in the stadium into raptures.

⚽ Boualem Khoukhi (QAT) — 90+5'  |  Switzerland scorer (TBC)
Brazil
1 – 1
Morocco
Group C · SoFi Stadium, Los Angeles
Vinícius Rescues Brazil After Saibari Stunner

In the most-anticipated match of the day, Morocco made a stunning statement on the world stage. The Atlas Lions — semi-finalists in Qatar four years ago and widely tipped as one of the tournament's dark horses — took the game to Brazil from the first whistle.

Ismael Saibari capitalised on hesitant defending to put Morocco ahead in the 21st minute, sparking wild celebrations among their passionate supporters. Brazil, five-time world champions, were rattled — but not for long. Star forward Vinícius Júnior struck back with a clinical finish in the 32nd minute to level, and neither side could find a winner in a match that had the crowd on its feet throughout.

"Morocco proved once again they belong at the very top of the game. This draw flatters no one — both teams deserved more."
⚽ Ismael Saibari (MAR) — 21'  |  ⚽ Vinícius Júnior (BRA) — 32'
Haiti
0 – 1
Scotland
Group C · Gillette Stadium, Boston
McGinn Goal Ends Scotland's 36-Year World Cup Win Drought

Scenes of utter jubilation greeted the final whistle as Scotland recorded their first World Cup victory since 1990, when they beat Sweden 2-1. The Tartan Army, returning to the tournament for the first time since the 1998 World Cup in France, made their mark on US soil.

Aston Villa midfielder John McGinn was the hero, getting a vital touch on a deflected shot that wrong-footed Haitian goalkeeper Johny Placide in the 28th minute. Scotland defended resolutely for the remaining hour, withstanding Haitian pressure to claim three precious points in Group C.

For Haiti, making only their second World Cup appearance, the tournament continues — but they face an uphill battle to progress with Brazil and Morocco still to come in the group.

⚽ John McGinn (SCO) — 28'
Australia
2 – 0
Türkiye
Group D · BC Place, Vancouver
Socceroos Dominate Türkiye in Convincing Group D Opener

Australia announced themselves at the 2026 World Cup in emphatic fashion, defeating a fancied Türkiye side 2-0 in Vancouver. The Socceroos, who have been steadily building as a footballing nation, were organised, direct, and ruthless on the counter-attack.

Young forward Nestory Irankunda broke the deadlock with a composed finish after a swift early counter, silencing the Turkish fans who had backed their side to qualify from a competitive Group D. Türkiye — who notably rested star Kenan Yildiz — pushed for an equalizer but were caught again when substitute Connor Metcalfe doubled the lead in the second half, sealing a famous three-point haul for the Socceroos.

⚽ Nestory Irankunda (AUS) — 1st half  |  ⚽ Connor Metcalfe (AUS) — 2nd half

Saturday's results have set up a series of fascinating group-stage permutations. In Group C, Brazil and Morocco share the spoils while Scotland leap above Haiti into second place. In Group D, Australia lead the way after Turkey's opening-day defeat. The World Cup is only just getting started — and it has already delivered drama, upsets, and moments to remember.
